The first published release of HealthWidgy: your Garmin data from Health Connect on the Android home screen, with a Today dashboard, history screens and 13 widgets. Requires Android 14 or newer.

### Install
Download **HealthWidgy-0.2.0.apk** below on your phone, open it and allow the install. On first start, a setup checklist walks you through Health Connect, Garmin Connect and the permissions.

If you built and installed HealthWidgy yourself before, uninstall that copy first: it is signed with a different key, so Android refuses to install this APK over it ("App not installed"). Uninstalling also removes the app's settings, its widgets on the home screen and its Health Connect permissions, so the setup checklist runs again and you add the widgets again. Your data in Health Connect is not affected.

### New in 0.2.0
- **English and Czech.** The app follows the phone's language, or you pick System, Čeština or English in *Settings → Language*. Numbers, dates and the widgets follow the choice too.
- **Your own Overview.** The edit button at the top of the Overview tab, next to refresh, opens *Edit overview*: which cards the tab shows, in which order, and whether each is half or full width. Two half cards side by side always have the same height.
- **Richer full-width cards:** heart rate with the day's hourly chart, sleep with its stages over the night, calories with the last 7 days and weight with the last 30 days.
- **Distance and floors cards**, each with its goal; at full width they also show the last 7 days. They are not on the Overview by default: add them in *Edit overview* under *Add a card*.
- **Two 1×1 widgets:** *Small ring* (one goal) and *Small value* (any metric with its icon).
- **A look for each widget.** Any widget can be light or dark regardless of the phone, for example a dark widget on a light home screen.
